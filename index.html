Technical support

How can we help?

Layla plans sleep around local prayer times and the things you need to wake for. If something feels wrong or unclear, tell us what you expected and what happened.

Contact

Email layla.app.support@gmail.com. Include your app language, the build shown in TestFlight and the approximate time of the issue.

Email Layla Support

Never send your backup recovery key or a raw Apple Health export. Remove private information from screenshots.

Before relying on an alarm

Review Today and Plan. Confirm your city, prayer times, commitments and enabled alarms. During beta testing, keep a separate alarm for essential wakes. If Layla says prayer times need updating, open it while connected before relying on prayer alarms.

Recorded sleep has not appeared

First check that the sleep record is visible in Apple Health. Then reopen Layla. Health synchronization and background execution are controlled by iOS, so new measurements may not appear immediately.

Backups

Save the recovery key somewhere outside Layla, confirm it is saved, choose a folder and check the Last Backup time. A successful file write does not by itself confirm that a cloud provider has finished syncing the file.

Privacy

Layla has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. Read the privacy policy for Health, location, prayer-time, backup and support details.
