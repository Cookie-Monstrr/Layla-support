Information in Layla

Settings, sleep observations, sleep scores, commitments, alarm choices and prayer records are stored on your device. Layla does not upload these records to a Layla-operated server.

Ahmed Elgamal is responsible for personal information handled directly for Layla, such as support correspondence. Where UK data protection law applies, that correspondence is handled for the legitimate interests of answering questions, investigating faults and improving Layla.

Apple Health

With your permission, Layla reads sleep records, heart-rate variability and resting heart rate from Apple Health to calculate sleep insights and support recovery planning. It does not write to Apple Health. Calculations happen on your device, and relevant observations and derived results are saved in Layla's history.

You can withdraw Health access through iOS. This stops further access but does not erase observations already saved in Layla. Layla does not sell Health data or use it for advertising.

Location and prayer times

You can select a city or permit location access. Apple location and geocoding services help identify your city and time zone.

To obtain prayer times, Layla sends the selected city, country, requested date and calculation settings to Aladhan. It does not include sleep history or missed-prayer counts. The provider also receives network information such as your IP address. Results are cached on your device.

Aladhan's operator says request information is used for security, monitoring, short-lived operations and traffic forecasting. It has not provided specific retention periods or processing locations in its public response to Layla's inquiry. See its service terms. We cannot promise a specific deletion time or that its processing takes place in the United Kingdom.

When you select the London Unified timetable, Layla reads the timetable included with the app rather than requesting those prayer times from Aladhan. Location and geocoding services may still be used if you enable them.

Widgets and local sharing

Layla stores plan summaries, progress summaries and derived sleep scores in a shared container on your device. Its widgets and other apps or extensions with permission to use that container can access those records. This is local sharing, not an internet upload.

Notifications and widgets may reveal sleep or prayer information on screen. You control notification previews and widget placement in iOS.

If you choose to share a card or image, the app or recipient you select receives that content under its own privacy arrangements. Sharing does not automatically send a copy to Layla support.

Backups and retention

Layla keeps recorded history beyond a graph's selected range. Local history remains until you delete the app or otherwise remove its data. Independent backup files remain until you delete them from the chosen folder.

Configured backups are encrypted before being written to your chosen folder. A file provider may sync them under its own terms. Automatic backups run when Layla gets an opportunity and the folder is available; a successful write does not guarantee cloud synchronization. Keep the recovery key private and separate from Layla.

A restore can also create a local encrypted safety copy. Deleting or replacing records in one place does not automatically erase independent backups, previously shared content or copies held by other services.

Support and TestFlight

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and chosen attachments. Gmail processes the correspondence. Avoid sending sensitive health or prayer records, and never send a recovery key. Google's Privacy Policy applies to its service.

Identifiable support emails and developer-held copies of TestFlight feedback are deleted within 90 days after the issue is resolved, unless needed for an unresolved issue or legal obligation. Anonymous development notes may remain. This does not control copies retained by Apple, Google or the sender.

Apple may provide the developer with beta crash, usage and feedback information. Apple's TestFlight privacy terms apply.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages have no added analytics, advertising, forms or external fonts.

Your choices and rights

You can manage permissions, notifications, widgets, backups and feedback. Support cannot remotely access your local Layla history.

Depending on the circumstances, UK law may give you rights concerning personal information held by support, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. Legal exceptions may apply. Email support to make a request. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Third-party services may process information outside the United Kingdom. Their own privacy information explains their arrangements; Aladhan's specific processing locations and transfer arrangements have not been confirmed to us.
